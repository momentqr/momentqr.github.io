Questions

Quick answers before you book.

Do guests need to download an app?

No. They scan the QR with the phone camera and the page opens in the browser.

Do guests need to log in?

No login needed. They just pick photos and tap upload.

Where do the photos go?

Into a Google Drive folder that belongs to you, in full quality. You can download everything after the party.

Can guests upload videos?

Yes, short clips (about 15 to 30 seconds).

How long before the party should I book?

At least 3 days before, so we can set up your page and print the cards.
