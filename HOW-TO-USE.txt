MOMENTQR - HOW IT WORKS NOW (NO CODE, JUST SAVE)
================================================

Studio (your control page):  https://momentqr.github.io/studio-4rgdtr/
PIN: the ADMIN_PIN in your Google script (default 2468)

NEW CLIENT
  1. Client creates a Google Drive folder and shares it with your Gmail as Editor.
  2. Studio > "+ New client" > fill name, date, theme, photo, background, song,
     paste the client's folder link > Save.
  3. Links & QR appear: guests' link (put on QR), TV slideshow link.
  4. "Make table cards (PDF)" > Save as PDF > print.

CHANGE A CLIENT
  Studio > Your clients > Edit > change anything > Save. Live in about 1 minute.

AFTER THE PARTY
  Edit > untick "Uploads and wishes are open" > Save.
  Photos + "Birthday Wishes" sheet are in the client's own folder.

BUSINESS HOME PAGE (momentqr.github.io)
  Studio > Business home page > WhatsApp + prices > Save business info.

WHERE THINGS LIVE
  Your Google Drive > "Momentqr Data"  = all client settings + photos/songs for pages
  Client's folder                      = guests' photos + wishes
  GitHub                               = the website (no more uploads needed)
